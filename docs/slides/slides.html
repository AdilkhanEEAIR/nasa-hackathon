The problem

Earth science reaches people almost only through the eyes

  • NASA's Earth Information Center tells the story of a changing planet with stunning visualizations, and only visualizations.
  • Globally, at least 2.2 billion people have a near or distance vision impairment (WHO, World report on vision, 2019). For many of them a map is silent.
  • Most sonification projects play a picture line by line. You hear a melody but not where things are.

Our question:

Can you navigate an Earth visualization by ear and by touch, and understand where the planet is bright, smoky, stormy, warm or cold?

Sixth Sense2 / 7

The solution

A sonar for Earth data: the shrinking ring

↑ High notescome from the north, low notes from the south
→ Right earis east, left ear is west
◉ Loudermeans more (light, dust, storms…)
♪ Timbresays what it is: each phenomenon has its own voice
  • Pick a place. 16 voices on a ring close in on it; at the end they meet in one note, the value at the centre.
  • The ring is computed on the sphere (great-circle distance), so it is true on Earth at any zoom.
  • Explore mode: walk a probe with the arrow keys; "Where am I" names the place.
  • All years: 2012→2023 as a melody. Save as WAV: your own Earth playlist.

Sixth Sense5 / 7

Accessibility and hardware

Keyboard, screen reader, and touch

  • No mouse needed: Space play · arrows move · W where am I · E explore.
  • An Accessibility switch comes first on every page (Alt+A): an optional voice guide, off by default and silent on tab changes, and a large-text, high-contrast mode.
  • Live status region for screen readers, sound tutorial (T), AA contrast, reduced motion respected, colour-blind-checked data colours.
  • Fast for everyone: the home page is 0.09 MB, the player 0.18 MB.

Feel it, with what people already have

  • Android phone vibration: pulses grow with the value under the scan.
  • Game controller rumble: heavy motor = south (low notes), light motor = north (high notes); the left stick moves the centre.
  • A Haptics switch, shown only where vibration works (not on iPhone).
